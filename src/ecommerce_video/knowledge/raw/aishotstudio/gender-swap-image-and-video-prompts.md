## Gender Swap Image Prompt. Best in Nano Banana.
Gender-swapped counterpart of the reference subject (opposite sex), retaining the main facial features, identity, and expressions, featuring a completely restructured torso volume and silhouette that strictly overrides the original body depth to ensure a biologically accurate, gender-appropriate chest structure, hand structure, hairstyle, facial hair, and other gender-appropriate details. Wearing wardrobe transformed into gender-appropriate tailoring, drape, and fashion, completely omitting gender-incongruent jewelry or accessories while preserving the original color palette, holding the original pose and body language adjusted naturally for the new anatomical frame, 100% identical background environment to the reference image devoid of text or typography, identical lighting setup matching original specular highlights and color temperature, photorealistic fidelity matching the original stylistic tone, exact identical camera framing, focal length, depth of field, and composition.

## Gender Swap Video Transformation Prompt. Best in Kling 2.5 or 3.0, 5 seconds long.
The initial subject fluidly morphs and transforms into the target subject, with physical characteristics, structural features, and clothing seamlessly shifting. The surrounding background environment dynamically blends and resolves to match the final state. Consistent natural lighting adapting smoothly to the new forms. Hyper-realistic, continuous fluid transition. Smooth handheld tracking, seamless temporal morphing, continuous video interpolation, 60fps.
