# AIShotStudio 26 Camera Angles & Shot Types（景别/机位提示词库）

> 来源：https://aishotstudio.com/26-camera-angles-shot-types/
> 说明：26 种景别/机位/视角提示词，英文原文。分三类：Shot Sizes、Camera Angles、Perspective & Lens。使用时需中文化。

## 景别（Shot Sizes）

| 景别 | 英文提示词 |
|---|---|
| 大远景 EWS | Extreme wide shot, establishing shot, vast landscape, tiny subject |
| 远景 WS | Wide shot, full body, long shot |
| 全景 FS | Full shot, full body visible, head to toe |
| 中远景 MLS | Medium long shot, framed from knees up, american shot, three-quarter length |
| 中景 MS | Medium shot, waist up, mid-shot |
| 中近景 MCU | Medium close-up, chest up, head and shoulders |
| 近景 CU | Close-up, face shot, tight framing |
| 大特写 ECU | Extreme close-up, macro eye, ECU, detail shot |
| 微距 Macro Shot | Macro photography, microscopic view, highly detailed texture, 100mm macro lens |

## 机位角度（Camera Angles）

| 角度 | 英文提示词 |
|---|---|
| 平视 Eye Level | Eye level shot, neutral angle, straight on, camera at eye height |
| 低角度 Low Angle | Low angle, looking up, imposing view, hero angle |
| 高角度 High Angle | High angle, looking down, inferior angle, subject looking up |
| 蠕虫视角 Worm's Eye View | Worm's eye view, ground level, looking straight up, ant's view |
| 鸟瞰/俯视 Bird's Eye View | Bird's eye view, overhead shot, top-down view, 90 degree angle |
| 上帝视角 God's Eye View | God's eye view, satellite view, high altitude, vertical angle |
| 荷兰角 Dutch Angle | Dutch angle, canted angle, tilted horizon, diagonal shot, disorienting |

## 视角与镜头（Perspective & Lens）

| 视角 | 英文提示词 |
|---|---|
| 第一人称 POV | POV, first-person perspective, seeing through eyes, hands visible |
| 过肩 OTS | Over-the-shoulder shot, looking past shoulder, blurry foreground shoulder, OTS |
| 双人镜 Two-Shot | Two-shot, 2 subjects in frame, medium shot, side-by-side |
| 鱼眼 Fisheye View | Fisheye lens, barrel distortion, ultra-wide 180 degree, vignette |
| 等距视角 Isometric View | Isometric view, orthographic projection, 3d render, diorama |
| 长焦压缩 Telephoto / Compression | Telephoto lens, long focal length, compressed background, flattened perspective |
| 自拍视角 Selfie View | Selfie angle, arm's length, front facing camera, phone camera distortion |
| 无人机视角 Drone View | Drone view, aerial photography, hovering, high altitude |
| 监控视角 CCTV / Security Cam | CCTV footage, security camera, surveillance view, grainy, high angle corner |
| 运动相机 GoPro / Action Cam | GoPro footage, action camera, body cam, wide angle, barrel distortion |
